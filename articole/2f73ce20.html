Commission approves €1 billion Slovenian State aid to support closure of Premogovnik Velenje coal mine

European Commission Press release Brussels, 23 Nov 2026 The European Commission has approved, under EU State aid rules, a €1 billion Slovenian measure to cover exceptional costs related to the closure of the Premogovnik Velenje coal mine.

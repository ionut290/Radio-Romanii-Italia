Russia’s FIMI and malicious cyber activities: Council imposes sanctions on 30 individuals and two entities

The Council adopted restrictive measures against 30 individuals and one entity responsible for actions on behalf of the Russian Federation undermining and threatening democracy, stability or security in the EU and some its Member States or in third countries.

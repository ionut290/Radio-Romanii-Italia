Commission partially greenlights Bulgaria's final payment request for €1.82 billion under NextGenerationEU

European Commission Press release Brussels, 20 Nov 2026 Today, the European Commission partially greenlighted Bulgaria's fifth and final payment request for €1.82 billion under the Recovery and Resilience Facility, the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU.

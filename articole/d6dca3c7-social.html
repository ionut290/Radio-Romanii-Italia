Commission greenlights the Netherlands' fifth and final payment request for €1.7 billion under NextGenerationEU

European Commission Press release Brussels, 19 Nov 2026 Today, the European Commission greenlighted the Netherlands' fifth and last payment request worth €1.7 billion under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU.

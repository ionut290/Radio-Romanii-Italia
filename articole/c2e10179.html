Commission partially greenlights Poland's final payment request for €12.7 billion under NextGenerationEU

European Commission Press release Brussels, 20 Nov 2026 Today, the European Commission partially greenlighted Poland's sixth and final payment request for €12.7 billion under the Recovery and Resilience Facility, the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU.

Commission greenlights Greece's final payment requests for €11.32 billion under NextGenerationEU

European Commission Press release Brussels, 20 Nov 2026 Today, the European Commission greenlighted Greece's eighth and ninth payment requests, amounting to €11.32 billion, under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU.

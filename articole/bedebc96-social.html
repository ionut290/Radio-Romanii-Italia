Commission partially greenlights Belgium's final payment request for €1.38 billion under NextGenerationEU

European Commission Press release Brussels, 20 Nov 2026 Today, the European Commission partially greenlighted Belgium's sixth and final payment request for €1.38 billion under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU.

Commission partially greenlights Croatia's final payment request for €2.67 billion under NextGenerationEU

European Commission Press release Brussels, 17 Nov 2026 Today, the European Commission partially greenlighted Croatia's ninth and final payment request worth €2.67 billion under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU.
